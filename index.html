04 Research

Thesis & Research

In Progress Undergraduate Thesis · Team Project

DigiDoc: A Privacy-Preserving Distributed Document Digitization Framework for Automated Form Transcription

A full-stack framework that digitizes scanned application forms without exposing a complete document to any single reviewer. Each form is split into field-level segments, scored for sensitivity, and distributed across independent verifiers, who transcribe their portion with in-browser OCR assistance before the results are merged into one structured record.

  1. IngestPDF forms rendered to page images
  2. AnnotateField regions marked on reusable templates
  3. ScoreEach field rated 1–10 for sensitivity
  4. DistributeSensitivity groups sent to different verifiers
  5. TranscribeOCR suggests text, verifiers confirm
  6. AggregateSegments merged into final data
  • Privacy by design: fields are grouped by sensitivity score and each group is assigned to a different verifier, so no single person sees the whole form whenever enough verifiers are available.
  • Semantic sensitivity scoring: field labels are matched against a 10-tier scale, from critical identifiers (NID, bank account) down to document metadata, using MiniLM sentence embeddings, with human override and a lightweight token-similarity fallback.
  • In-browser OCR evaluation: Tesseract.js (WebAssembly) runs three preprocessing pipelines, including upscaling with Otsu binarization, compared against human transcriptions using CER, WER and Levenshtein edit analysis.
  • Workload-aware distribution: segment batches are cropped from source pages and assigned to the least-loaded eligible verifier.
  • Multi-tenant access control: organizations and branches with Super Admin, Branch Admin and Verifier roles, secured with JWT access/refresh tokens.
  • Deployment: containerized with Docker Compose; CI/CD with GitHub Actions, frontend on Vercel and backend on Render.
